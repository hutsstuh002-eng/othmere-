STANDARDS

Quality matters at every stage.

Othmere operates around clear standards for research, qualification, communication, and the opportunities we deliver.

Relevance

Prospects should have a genuine relationship to the service being offered. We do not treat every contact as a useful opportunity simply because a database says it exists.

Accuracy

Research and prospect information should be reviewed before it is used in the acquisition process.

Qualification

Interest and relevance are evaluated before an opportunity is considered ready for the next stage.

Communication

Outreach should be relevant, professional, and appropriate to the person receiving it.

Transparency

Businesses should have a clear understanding of what is being delivered, how opportunities are generated, and what happens at each stage.

Human Oversight

Automation can improve speed and consistency, but important decisions remain subject to human review.

THE PRINCIPLE

More activity does not automatically mean better acquisition.

Our objective is not to maximize the number of messages sent or contacts collected. It is to create a reliable process for finding and developing relevant business opportunities.
