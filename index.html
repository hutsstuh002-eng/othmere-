THE ROLE OF OTHMERE

Requirements are often clearer than their solutions.

A business may know what it needs without having an established way to obtain it. The relevant expertise may not exist internally, the appropriate specialist may not be known to the organization, or the requirement may simply fall outside its normal operations.

Othmere exists to handle that gap.

A requirement is considered according to what is actually needed, and an appropriate external capability is identified and engaged where suitable.

THE PROCESS

A requirement begins with the requirement itself.

01

Requirement

The business describes what it needs, the intended outcome and any relevant conditions or constraints.

02

Assessment

The requirement is examined to establish the capability, qualifications, scope and practical conditions involved.

03

Fulfillment

An appropriate external capability is identified and the engagement is arranged according to the requirement.

04

Delivery

The selected organization performs the relevant work under the terms established for that engagement.

STANDARDS

The capability matters as much as the requirement.

External organizations considered for a requirement are assessed according to factors relevant to the work involved, including demonstrated capability, experience, qualifications, operational suitability and the specific conditions of the requirement.

The objective is not to provide an arbitrary selection of organizations, but to determine whether a particular capability is appropriate for a particular requirement.

RESPONSIBILITY

Othmere's role has defined limits.

Othmere assesses requirements, arranges appropriate external capabilities and establishes the conditions under which an engagement is undertaken. The substantive service itself is performed by the organization engaged for that purpose.

Othmere's involvement does not transfer the professional, contractual, regulatory or operational responsibilities applicable to the organization performing the work.

EXTERNAL CAPABILITIES

Organizations capable of fulfilling relevant requirements.

Othmere works with independent organizations capable of fulfilling particular requirements. Organizations are considered according to relevant capability, experience, qualifications, operational suitability and the conditions of the work involved.
