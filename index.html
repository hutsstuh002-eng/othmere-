THE ROLE OF OTHMERE

A requirement is assessed according to what is actually needed.

01

Requirement

A business identifies what it requires, the intended outcome and any conditions that matter.

02

Assessment

The requirement is examined to establish the capability, qualifications and practical conditions involved.

03

Fulfillment

An appropriate external organization is identified and the engagement is arranged according to the requirement.

OPERATING FRAMEWORK

A defined role between the requirement and the capability.

01

Requirements

Business requirements are considered according to their nature, intended outcome, scope and conditions.

02

Standards

External capabilities are considered according to relevant experience, qualifications, demonstrated capability and operational suitability.

03

Role

Othmere assesses requirements, identifies appropriate capabilities and establishes the conditions of the engagement.
