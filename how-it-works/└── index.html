HOW IT WORKS

A structured process from prospect to opportunity.

Othmere combines research, qualification, outreach, and human involvement to create a consistent path toward new business.

01

Research

We identify businesses and decision-makers that match the characteristics of your ideal client.

02

Verify

Potential prospects are reviewed to make sure they are relevant before outreach begins.

03

Reach Out

Prospects are contacted through a structured outreach process designed around relevance rather than volume alone.

04

Qualify

Responses and interest are assessed so genuine opportunities can be separated from simple replies or poor-fit prospects.

05

Connect

Qualified opportunities are moved toward a direct conversation with the appropriate business.

06

Close

Your team takes over the sales conversation and handles the final decision-making process.

BUILT AROUND PEOPLE

Automation handles repetition. People handle judgment.

Technology helps us research, organize, and manage large amounts of information. Human oversight remains where context, judgment, and communication matter.
